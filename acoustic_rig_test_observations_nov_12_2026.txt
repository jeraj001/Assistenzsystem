2. AUDIO & SPEAKER SYSTEM OBSERVATIONS
--------------------------------------
- Volume Imbalance Between Speakers:
  * The main (active) speaker—which receives power directly and passes the
    second audio channel to the passive speaker—is significantly louder than
    the secondary speaker.
  * Even the main speaker was quieter than expected overall.
- Suspected Cable Attenuation Cause:
  * The physical span of the setup is ~4–5 m max, yet a 30+ m (estimated ~50 m)
    cable is currently routed between the primary and secondary speakers.
  * Signal degradation over the excessive cable length likely accounts for the
    severe volume drop on the passive speaker.
- Action Items:
  * Measure exact SPL levels of both speakers using a decibel (dB) meter.
  * Shorten the speaker interconnect cable to match the ~4–5 m setup width,
    add an external amplifier, or evaluate an alternative speaker setup.

4. MECHANICAL & STEPPER MOTOR LIMITATIONS (CRITICAL)
----------------------------------------------------
- Insufficient Torque / Step Skipping:
  * During the 30-degree sweep test (90° -> 60°), the stepper motors stalled
    and skipped steps (similar to an over-torqued drill clutch), moving the
    speaker carts by only ~10–20 mm instead of the full 30° arc.
  * Note: Power supply voltage during this check was 12 V (needs verification
    at higher rated voltage/current, though mechanical load remains a primary
    concern).
- Extension Rod Bending & Load Dynamics:
  * Two central stepper motors drive ~1.5 m extension rods (matte, lightweight
    —likely aluminum or aluminum alloy) to maintain the 1.5 m speaker-to-mic
    radius.
  * Under load, torque fails to transmit along the full 1.5 m span (effective
    rigidity breaks down under 1 m), causing the rods to visibly bow/bend near
    the middle.
  * High rolling/static friction on the carpeted ("rug") floor—combined with
    the downward weight of the speaker, wheels, and mounting hardware—exceeds
    the torque the current motor-and-rod linkage can overcome.
- Action Items:
  * Verify if incomplete setup or 12 V supply limit contributed to the stall,
    and test at higher voltage/current if safe.
  * Re-evaluate the mechanical drive architecture: upgrade to higher-torque
    motors/gearboxes, stiffen or redesign the 1.5 m linkage to prevent bending,
    or transition to a self-propelled/wheeled mobile cart drive system.
